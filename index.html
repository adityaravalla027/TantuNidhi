Executive Summary

WeaveAhead turns scattered market signals into simple weekly forecasts delivered via WhatsApp and voice calls in regional languages. Visual icons and audio guidance completely bypass app literacy barriers[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

The Core Problem

1. Unpredictable Demand

Seasonal swings, festival calendars, and shifting buyer trends leave weavers guessing what to produce[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

2. Delayed Payments

No visibility into order cycles or cash flow makes monthly household income impossible to plan[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

3. Unsold Inventory

Weaving without demand signals leads directly to wasted yarn materials and locked-up working capital[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

4. Youth Exodus

Financial instability pushes the next generation away from ancestral handloom craft entirely[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

How It Works

1. Collect Market Signals

Pulls local mandi market data, wedding calendars, e-commerce trends, and coop records[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

2. Forecast Demand

AI predicts specific product category demand: Sarees, Stoles, or Home Furnishings[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).

3. Simplify for Low-Literacy

Converts raw numbers into visual icons, color bars, and regional voice messages[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

4. Deliver Alerts

Dispatches weekly SMS and WhatsApp voice messages directly to basic phones[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
